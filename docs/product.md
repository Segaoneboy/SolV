# Product Vision: SolV

## What is SolV?
SolV is a decentralized infrastructure protocol built on Solana for the tokenization of real-world  assets (RWAs), specifically programmable vouchers, tickets, and gift certificates.

## Target Audience
1. **Small Businesses**: Coffee shops, gyms, and local services looking to avoid high merchant fees.
2. **Web3 Native Brands**: Launching limited-edition physical merchandise or event access.
3. **Corporate Wellness**: Companies issuing burnout-prevention vouchers for employees


## Problems
Traditional pre-paid asset systems (gift cards, vouchers, service credits) suffer from:
- **High Fees**: Merchants lose 3-15% to middlemen and banking fees.
- **Slow Settlement**: Funds take 2-5 days to reach the merchant's account.
- **Lack of Transparency**: Users can't easily verify the validity of a voucher on a public ledger.
- **Fragile Infrastructure**: Centralized databases are prone to downtime and data breaches.


## Key Value Propositions:
- **Instant Liquidity**: Merchants receive funds the moment a voucher is purchased.
- **Programmable Trust**: Funds are held in a secure Solana Vault and only released/accounted for upon valid redemption.
- **Low-Friction Entry**: Using Privy, users can buy RWA vouchers using social logins, completely bypassing the "wallet hurdle."
- **Verifiable Scarcity**: Every voucher is a unique PDA, preventing double-spending or counterfeit gift cards.
